# SOIL HEALTH CARD SCHEME (SHC) & FERTILIZER APPLICATION NORMS
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW), Government of India  
**Objective:** Promoting balanced and judicious use of plant nutrients through periodic soil fertility assessments.

---

### Soil Testing Cycle and Card Issuance
* **Cycle Duration:** Soil Health Cards are distributed to all farmers across India at an interval of **every 3 years** (previously conducted on a 2-year grid cycle).
* **Grid Sampling Protocol:**
  - In irrigated areas: Soil samples drawn from a grid of **2.5 hectares**.
  - In rainfed/dryland areas: Soil samples drawn from a grid of **10.0 hectares**.
* **Delivery:** Delivered physically and digitally accessible on the Soil Health portal (`soilhealth.dac.gov.in`).

---

### The 12 Mandatory Soil Quality Parameters
Each Soil Health Card assesses soil fertility across **12 specific chemical and physical parameters**:
1. **Primary Macro-Nutrients (3):**
   - Nitrogen (N)
   - Phosphorus (P)
   - Potassium (K)
2. **Secondary Macro-Nutrient (1):**
   - Sulphur (S)
3. **Micro-Nutrients (5):**
   - Zinc (Zn)
   - Iron (Fe)
   - Copper (Cu)
   - Manganese (Mn)
   - Boron (B)
4. **Physical / Chemical Indicators (3):**
   - pH (Acidity or Alkalinity level)
   - Electrical Conductivity (EC - indicator of soil salinity)
   - Organic Carbon (OC - measure of biological soil fertility)

---

### Soil Testing Laboratories & Entrepreneurship Subsidies
* **Village-Level Soil Testing Laboratories (VLSTL):**
  - **Eligible Entities:** Rural youth, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), PACS, and rural entrepreneurs aged 18 to 40 years.
  - **Project Cost:** Standard capital cost benchmark is **Rs. 5.00 Lakhs**.
  - **Subsidy Rate:** **75% financial assistance** (maximum grant of **Rs. 3.75 Lakhs**) provided by the Government.
  - **Beneficiary Contribution:** Remaining 25% (Rs. 1.25 Lakhs) invested by the entrepreneur or through bank credit.
* **Mini Labs / Mobile Testing Units:**
  - Financial assistance of **100% up to Rs. 2.50 Lakhs** for ICAR / KVK labs; **50% up to Rs. 1.25 Lakhs** for village entrepreneurs.

---

### Fertilizer Recommendation Norms
* Fertilizer dosages provided on the card are customized for 6 main local crops based on Soil Test Crop Response (STCR) equations.
* Promotes 4R Nutrient Stewardship: Right source, Right rate, Right time, and Right place.
