# PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN URJA SURAKSHA EVAM UTTHAAN MAHABHIYAN (PM-KUSUM)
**Ministry:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India  
**Objective:** Providing energy security to farmers, de-dieselization of farm irrigation, and increasing farmer income through grid-connected solar power plants.

---

### Component-A: Decentralized Ground/Stilt Mounted Grid-Connected Solar Plants
* **Target Capacity:** 10,000 MW aggregate solar or other renewable energy capacity.
* **Plant Capacity Range:** 500 kW to 2 MW per project (can be 250 kW in specific cases).
* **Eligible Applicants:** Individual farmers, groups of farmers, cooperatives, panchayats, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and Water User Associations (WUAs).
* **Land Requirement:** Approximately 4 to 5 acres of cultivable or uncultivable/barren land per 1 MW capacity.
* **Power Evacuation & Tariff:** The generated electricity is purchased by the local Distribution Company (DISCOM) at a Feed-in-Tariff (FiT) determined by the State Electricity Regulatory Commission (SERC).
* **Leasing Provisions:** If the farmer lacks investment capital, they can lease their land to a solar developer with advance lease rent negotiated directly or fixed by the state authority.

---

### Component-B: Standalone Solar Agriculture Pumps (Off-Grid)
* **Scope:** Installation of individual standalone solar agricultural pumps of capacity up to 7.5 HP for irrigation in off-grid areas.
* **Eligible Pump Capacities:** Pumps higher than 7.5 HP can also be installed, but Central Financial Assistance (CFA) is strictly capped at the 7.5 HP benchmark rate.
* **Subsidy Structure (General States):**
  - **Central Government Subsidy (CFA):** 30% of the benchmark cost.
  - **State Government Subsidy:** Minimum 30% of the benchmark cost.
  - **Farmer Contribution:** Maximum 40% of the cost.
  - **Bank Loan Availability:** Farmers can avail bank loans for up to 30% of the total cost, requiring only 10% upfront margin money from the farmer.
* **Subsidy Structure (Special Category States - NE States, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, J&K, Ladakh, Goa, and Island territories):**
  - **Central Government Subsidy (CFA):** 50% of the benchmark cost.
  - **State Government Subsidy:** Minimum 30% of the benchmark cost.
  - **Farmer Contribution:** Maximum 20% of the cost.
* **Groundwater Safeguards:** Standalone solar pumps are only permitted to replace existing diesel pumps or for new irrigation in blocks categorized as 'Safe' by the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB). Installation in 'Over-exploited' / 'Critical' / 'Dark' groundwater zones is prohibited for new tubewells.

---

### Component-C: Solarisation of Existing Grid-Connected Agriculture Pumps
* **Individual Pump Solarisation (IPS):** Solar PV panels installed at farmer's tubewell. Farmer satisfies irrigation needs and sells surplus power back to DISCOM.
  - Central Financial Assistance: 30% of benchmark cost.
  - State Subsidy: 30%.
  - Farmer Contribution: 40% (or supported by bank finance).
* **Feeder Level Solarisation (FLS):** Instead of individual farm solarisation, dedicated agricultural feeders are solarised at the distribution substation level.
  - CFA: Rs. 1.05 Crore per MW for feeder solarisation.

---

### Application and Grievance Redressal
* **Portal:** Official National PM-KUSUM Portal (`pmkusum.mnre.gov.in`) or designated State Nodal Agency (SNA) portal.
* **Warning against Fraud:** Farmers must not pay registration fees on unauthorized or counterfeit websites; applications are strictly processed via state implementing agencies.
