# PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY) OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW), Government of India  
**Objective:** Financial support to farmers suffering crop loss/damage arising out of unforeseen natural calamities, stabilizing income, and ensuring flow of agricultural credit.

---

### Coverage of Farmers
* **Voluntary Scheme:** Since Kharif 2020, enrollment under PMFBY is completely voluntary for all farmers (including loanee farmers with Kisan Credit Cards and non-loanee farmers).
* **Tenant & Sharecropper Farmers:** Tenant farmers and sharecroppers cultivating eligible land are fully entitled to insurance coverage upon providing sowing certificates or tenancy agreements as prescribed by the respective State Government.

---

### Premium Rates Payable by Farmers (Actuarial Ceilings)
Farmers pay only a nominal uniform maximum premium rate, while the entire balance actuarial premium is split equally (50:50) between the Central and State Governments (90:10 for North-Eastern States):
* **Kharif Season (All Foodgrains and Oilseeds crops):** Maximum **2.0%** of Sum Insured.
* **Rabi Season (All Foodgrains and Oilseeds crops):** Maximum **1.5%** of Sum Insured.
* **Commercial & Annual Horticultural Crops (Kharif & Rabi):** Maximum **5.0%** of Sum Insured.

---

### Stages of Risk & Covered Perils
1. **Prevented Sowing / Planting / Germination Risk:** Insured area prevented from sowing due to deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal conditions. Indemnity payable up to 25% of the Sum Insured.
2. **Standing Crop Losses (Yield Loss):** Comprehensive risk insurance covering yield losses due to non-preventable risks such as drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, widespread pest & disease attacks, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm, and cyclone.
3. **Mid-Season Adversity:** In case of severe drought or floods where expected yield is less than 50% of normal, on-account immediate relief payment of **up to 25%** of likely claims is disbursed before final harvest.
4. **Post-Harvest Losses:** Coverage available for up to a maximum period of **14 days** from harvesting for crops kept in "cut and spread" / small bundled condition in the field for drying against specific perils of hailstorm, cyclone, cyclonic rains, and unseasonal rains.
5. **Localized Calamities:** Loss or damage resulting from occurrence of identified localized risks of hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst, and natural fire affecting isolated farms.

---

### Mandatory Claim Intimation Window (Crucial 72-Hour Rule)
* **Intimation Deadline:** For localized calamities and post-harvest losses, individual intimation MUST be submitted by the affected farmer **within 72 hours** of the occurrence of the calamity.
* **Reporting Channels:**
  1. National Crop Insurance Portal (NCIP) via the official **Crop Insurance Mobile App**.
  2. National Crop Insurance Toll-Free Helpline: **14447**.
  3. Direct written notice to the local financial institution / bank branch through which the policy was taken.
  4. Local Agriculture Department Office (Gram Panchayat Agriculture Assistant / Krishi Rakshak).
* **Consequence of Delay:** Intimations submitted after 72 hours are subject to rejection if ground verification becomes unverifiable by the Loss Assessment Joint Committee.

---

### Excluded Risks & Non-Payable Perils
* Losses arising out of war, nuclear risks, malicious damage, theft, pilferage, cattle grazing, or deliberate sabotage are strictly excluded.
* Post-harvest loss coverage does NOT apply once the produce is moved from the farm field to a barn, storehouse, godown, or processed.
