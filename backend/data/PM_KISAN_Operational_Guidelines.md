# PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN) OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW), Government of India  
**Objective:** Augmenting the income of small and marginal farmers to meet domestic requirements and procurement of farm inputs.

---

### Financial Benefit and Installment Cycle
* **Benefit Amount:** Direct cash transfer of **Rs. 6,000/- per annum** per eligible farmer family.
* **Payment Mode:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts through PFMS (Public Financial Management System).
* **Installment Schedule:** Paid in **three equal four-monthly installments of Rs. 2,000/- each**:
  1. Period 1: April to July
  2. Period 2: August to November
  3. Period 3: December to March

---

### Definition of Beneficiary Family & Landholding Rules
* **Family Unit:** A landholder farmer's family is defined as a family consisting of **husband, wife, and minor children** who own cultivable land as per the land records of the concerned State/UT.
* **Land Ceiling:** There is **no upper ceiling** on landholding size (the original 2-hectare ceiling was eliminated in June 2019 to cover all landholding farmers).
* **Cut-Off Date:** The ownership of land must have been recorded before the cut-off date of **February 1, 2019**. Land transfers after February 1, 2019 are not eligible for benefits for a period of 5 years, except in cases of inheritance due to death of the landholder.

---

### Categories of Ineligible Persons (Exclusion Criteria)
The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status are **strictly excluded** from PM-KISAN benefits:
1. **Institutional Landholders:** Religious trusts, corporate farming entities, institutions owning agricultural land.
2. **Constitutional Positions:** Former and present holders of constitutional posts (Presidents, Governors, Judges).
3. **Political Office Bearers:** Former and present Ministers, Ministers of State, Members of Parliament (Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha), Members of Legislative Assemblies (MLAs), Members of Legislative Councils (MLCs), Mayors of Municipal Corporations, and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
4. **Government Employees:** All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Departments, PSEs, and Autonomous Institutions (NOTE: Multi-Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees ARE eligible and not excluded).
5. **High Pensioners:** All superannuated or retired pensioners whose monthly pension is **Rs. 10,000/- or more** (excluding Group D / MTS employees).
6. **Income Tax Payees:** All persons who paid Income Tax in the previous assessment year.
7. **Professionals:** Practicing professionals registered with professional bodies, specifically Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants (CAs), and Architects.

---

### Mandatory Compliance Steps for Disbursal
* **Mandatory e-KYC:** Beneficiaries must complete e-KYC via OTP authentication using Aadhaar or biometric/facial recognition through the PM-KISAN mobile app.
* **Land Seeding:** Land records must be verified and seeded in the state revenue portal linked to PM-KISAN.
* **Aadhaar-NPCI Bank Seeding:** The receiving bank account must be actively linked to the NPCI mapper for Aadhaar payment bridge.
