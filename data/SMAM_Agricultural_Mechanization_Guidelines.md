# SUB-MISSION ON AGRICULTURAL MECHANIZATION (SMAM) & KISAN DRONE GUIDELINES
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (MoA&FW), Government of India  
**Objective:** Increasing reach of farm mechanization to small and marginal farmers and regions where availability of farm power is low.

---

### Individual Farm Machinery Subsidies
* **Eligible Equipment:** Tractors, power tillers, rotavators, multi-crop threshers, laser land levelers, zero-till seed-cum-fertilizer drills, self-propelled combine harvesters.
* **Subsidy Rates for Individual Procurement:**
  - **Special Categories (SC, ST, Small & Marginal Farmers, Women, and NE States):** Up to **50% of the cost** or the specified financial ceiling (e.g., up to Rs. 1.25 Lakhs for power tillers, Rs. 1.00 Lakh for laser levelers).
  - **Other / General Category Farmers:** Up to **40% of the cost** or the specified financial ceiling.

---

### Custom Hiring Centres (CHC) for Farm Machinery
* **Purpose:** Enables small and marginal farmers who cannot afford costly machinery to hire modern equipment on a pay-per-use rental basis.
* **CHC Project Cost Ceiling:** Up to Rs. 25.00 Lakhs per centre.
* **Financial Assistance / Subsidy Rate:**
  - Up to **40% of the total project cost** (maximum subsidy of **Rs. 10.00 Lakhs**) provided to rural youth entrepreneurs, cooperatives, and registered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
  - For North-Eastern Region and Hill States, financial assistance is up to **60%** (maximum Rs. 15.00 Lakhs).

---

### High-Tech Hubs & Farm Machinery Banks (FMB)
* **Farm Machinery Banks (FMB) by SHGs / Panchayats:** In low mechanization districts, Self Help Groups (SHGs) and Village Panchayats receive up to **80% financial assistance** for machinery banks costing up to Rs. 10.00 Lakhs (maximum subsidy Rs. 8.00 Lakhs).
* **High-Tech Hubs for High-Value Machines:** Projects up to Rs. 2.50 Crores receive financial assistance of **40%** (up to Rs. 1.00 Crore).

---

### Kisan Drone Subsidies & Operational Rules
* **100% Grant (Free of Cost):** Up to **100% of the drone cost** (maximum **Rs. 10.00 Lakhs**) provided as grant-in-aid to ICAR institutes, Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs), State Agricultural Universities (SAUs), and ICAR-affiliated research centers for drone demonstrations on farmers' fields.
* **Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs):** Up to **75% grant** of the drone cost for conducting demonstrations.
* **Individual Farmer Purchase Subsidies:**
  - SC/ST, Small & Marginal farmers, Women, and NE State farmers: **50% of drone cost** (capped at maximum **Rs. 5.00 Lakhs**).
  - Other general category farmers: **40% of drone cost** (capped at maximum **Rs. 4.00 Lakhs**).
* **Custom Hiring Centres Buying Drones:** 40% of drone cost or maximum Rs. 4.00 Lakhs.
